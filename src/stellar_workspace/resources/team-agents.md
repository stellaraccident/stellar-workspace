# Team boundaries

Each named agent owns its directory and working checkouts. These are cooperative
boundaries, not ACLs. Stay in your own area unless the human or owning peer grants
permission, including through a bead assigned to inspect/triage that area.
Record scope and hand the findings back. Do not reset, clean, stash or switch
another agent's dirty checkout.

Use `sw-agent-readme team` and `sw-agent-readme machine` for installed details.
Read your named AGENTS.md, role file and both MOTDs. Do not commit root metadata,
push, merge, grant direnv trust or change host administration without a request.
GitHub/PyPI credentials stay outside the sandbox; do not bypass their boundary.
